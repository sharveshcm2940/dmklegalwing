# DMK Legal Wing — Design Philosophy

## The Big Idea
The DMK Legal Wing is not a generic legal association; it is a historic bar of advocates born from the Dravidian movement, defending constitutional federalism, social justice, and the 69% reservation in India's highest courts. Its visual language rejects modern corporate web templates in favor of authentic institutional craft: the printed political handbill, the high court cause list, and the rising sun emblem. Typography is deliberate and heavy on warm parchment paper, structured by razor-sharp 1 to 3 px rules and punctuated by a singular, unapologetic red.

## Singular Signature Moments
To ensure visual discipline without template clutter, every page is anchored by exactly ONE memorable signature element while everything else remains restrained and quiet:

1. **Home**: The slowly turning drawn sun (760 px, 160 s rotation with parallax depth) behind the tactile, tilted (-1.6°) parchment dispatch clipping with solid offset shadow.
2. **News (`/news`)**: The dual-ruled broadsheet masthead with giant Kavivanar "செய்திகள்" beside the live wire, accompanied by the dropped parchment date stamp in the lead communiqué.
3. **Resources (`/resources`)**: The dense institutional library catalogue table featuring live keyword letter-marking, dynamic counter, and an instant 0.4 s sliding docket preview drawer.
